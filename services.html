What we build

Choose the outcome you need

Most projects fit one of these four starting points. Many combine two.

Time

Automate manual processes

If your team copies the same information between spreadsheets, emails and forms every week, that work can run on its own. We build the system that does it, and flag anything that still needs a person to check.

Typical examples

  • Emailed or paper forms that land in the right place automatically
  • Reminders, follow-ups and weekly summaries sent without anyone remembering to
  • Several spreadsheets combined into one clean, always-current sheet

Systems

Custom management systems

Ready-made software often makes you change how you work. We build a system shaped around how your business already runs, so your team picks it up quickly.

Typical examples

  • Customers, orders or appointments managed in one place
  • Staff tasks and approvals with clear ownership
  • Member, student or client records with search and history

Cost

Billing & inventory automation

Manual invoices and stock counts quietly cost hours and cause errors that are expensive to find later. We automate the calculations and record-keeping, so month end becomes a review instead of a rebuild.

Typical examples

  • Invoices created from orders, with totals and tax worked out for you
  • Stock levels that update as items sell, with low-stock alerts
  • Payment tracking that shows who has paid and who is overdue
Discuss this →
Access

Internal tools & dashboards

When information is spread across several tools, someone always has to go looking for it. We bring what matters onto one screen that anyone on your team can read without training.

Typical examples

  • A live view of sales, stock or jobs in progress
  • Simple admin screens so non-technical staff can update data safely
  • Separate access for owners, staff and contractors, so each person sees only what they need
