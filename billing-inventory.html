Services / Billing & inventory

Make invoices and stock easier to keep in sync

Manual stock counts and invoices assembled from separate records make month-end harder than it needs to be. A connected workflow can keep orders, stock changes, invoices, and payment status together.

Connected records

Follow an order from sale to payment

The exact workflow depends on how you sell, price, and replenish products. Common pieces include:

Orders and invoices

Create an invoice from agreed order details, with consistent line items and calculations for review.

Stock movement

Record incoming and outgoing items, see current quantities, and flag stock that needs attention.

Payment status

Track what is paid, pending, or overdue so follow-up does not depend on memory.

Accuracy first

Automate calculations, keep review visible

Billing systems need to be dependable and easy to check. We agree on product, tax, discount, and stock rules before building, and make corrections traceable rather than hiding them behind automation.

  1. Confirm your rules

    Review how you price items, handle taxes, returns, discounts, and stock adjustments.

  2. Connect the steps

    Define how an order updates stock, produces an invoice, and records payment status.

  3. Test real scenarios

    Check ordinary orders alongside returns, partial payments, and manual corrections.

  4. Train and hand over

    Walk through daily use and explain how to verify totals and resolve exceptions.
