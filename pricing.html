Pricing

A clear price before work begins

Every project has a different scope. We learn how your business works, agree what is included, and send a written proposal with the price and expected timeline before any build work starts.

Ask about your project See how projects run

How engagements are priced

The approach depends on the work

We choose a pricing approach that fits the amount of certainty and flexibility a project needs.

Defined project scope

When the requirements are clear, the proposal sets out a fixed scope, agreed price, milestones, and what is not included.

Work that may evolve

For ongoing or exploratory work, the proposal explains the rate, how time is tracked, and when progress and spend are reviewed.

Changes and support

Requested changes are priced and approved before work begins. Post-launch fixes or support are included only when the proposal says so.

No surprises

What you will see in a proposal

Before you decide, you will have the information needed to understand the commitment. If the scope is not clear enough to estimate responsibly, we will explain what needs to be clarified first.

  • Deliverables, exclusions, and assumptions
  • Price or hourly rate, plus the payment schedule
  • Expected timeline, milestones, and what we need from you
  • How changes, launch, handover, and any support are handled

Start with a conversation, not a commitment

An enquiry is free and does not commit either of us. Tell us what you want to improve and we will suggest a sensible next step.
