Privacy Policy

What we collect, why we collect it, and what you can ask us to do with it.

Last updated: 24 November 2026

The short version. We collect only what you send us through the contact form or by email, and we use it to reply to you. We do not sell your details, we do not add you to a mailing list, and this site does not use advertising or analytics cookies.

1. Who we are

This site is run by HarshaForge Digital Solutions ("HarshaForge", "we", "us"), a small software and automation studio based in India. For anything in this policy, you can reach us at hello@harshaforge.com or +91 83284 83044.

2. What we collect

We collect information in two ways.

  • What you send us. The contact form asks for your name, email address, company, the kind of help you need, an optional budget range, and a description of your project. If you email or call us instead, we keep what you share in that conversation.
  • What your browser sends automatically. Like any website, our server may record your IP address, browser type and the pages requested. We use this for security and troubleshooting.

Please do not put passwords, payment card numbers or confidential business records in the form. If we need to see sensitive material, we will agree a safer way to share it first.

3. How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and discuss your project.
  • To prepare a proposal or quote if you ask for one.
  • To limit spam and repeated submissions, using your IP address briefly.
  • To keep the site secure and working.
  • To meet legal or accounting obligations if we work together.

We do not use your details for marketing unless you ask us to.

4. Who sees it

We do not sell or rent your information. It is handled only by us and the services we rely on to run the studio:

  • Email. Enquiries reach us as email, which is hosted by Google (Gmail).
  • Hosting. Our hosting provider serves this site and handles its server logs.
  • Fonts. This site loads its typeface from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google when a page loads.

We may also disclose information where the law requires it.

5. Cookies and tracking

We do not set advertising or analytics cookies, and we do not use tracking pixels. If that changes, we will update this page before it does.

6. How long we keep it

If an enquiry does not lead to a project, we keep it for up to 24 months in case you come back, then delete it. If we work together, we keep project records for as long as the engagement runs and for the period needed for accounting and legal purposes afterwards.

7. How we protect it

The site is served over HTTPS, form input is checked on our server as well as in your browser, and access to enquiries is limited to us. No system is perfectly secure, so we keep what we hold to a minimum.

8. Your choices

You can ask us at any time to:

  • tell you what information we hold about you,
  • correct anything that is wrong,
  • delete your information, or
  • stop contacting you.

Email hello@harshaforge.com and we will respond within a reasonable time, usually within two working days. We may need to keep some records where the law requires it.

9. Children

This site is for businesses. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. If a change affects how we use information you have already sent us, we will tell you by email.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy are welcome. Write to hello@harshaforge.com or use the contact page. You can also read our Terms of Service.
