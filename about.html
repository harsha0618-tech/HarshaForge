About

A small studio, run by the person who builds your system

HarshaForge builds software that saves businesses time and money, and keeps it simple enough for anyone on your team to use.

Our story

Why HarshaForge exists

Most small and mid-sized businesses lose hours every week to work a computer could do: retyping orders, reconciling stock, chasing numbers across spreadsheets. The software that would fix it is often either too generic to fit the way you work, or built and priced for much larger organizations.

HarshaForge was started to sit between the two: custom systems shaped around how your business already runs, delivered by a small studio you can actually reach.

Every project is measured against three questions. Does it save time? Does it lower cost? Is it simple enough that your team can use it without a manual?

Why trust us

No relay between you and the builder

In larger agencies, your message often passes through two or three people before it reaches whoever writes the code. Details get lost on the way. Here it does not.

How we work

What you can hold us to

Direct access

Your questions go to the person writing the code, so answers are specific rather than passed along.

Plain language

We explain what we are building, why, and what it will cost, without technical jargon.

Honest labeling

Concept builds are labeled as concepts. We do not show invented client names or made-up testimonials.

Built to be handed over

Systems are documented and handed over cleanly, so you are never locked in to depending on us.
