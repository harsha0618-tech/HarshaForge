From current workflow to launch

Build around real tasks, not assumptions

We use your current process as the starting point. Together, we choose a focused first version, test it with realistic records, and leave room to improve it as the team starts using it.

  1. Understand the records

    Agree on what information matters, who updates it, and how it is used.

  2. Design everyday screens

    Plan the searches, forms, lists, and summaries your team needs most.

  3. Check roles and edge cases

    Test permissions, incomplete records, corrections, and common hand-offs.

  4. Prepare the handover

    Provide a usable guide and walk through routine updates with your team.
