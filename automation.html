How we approach it

Make the process clearer before automating it

Automation should remove unnecessary steps, not make a confusing process happen faster. We map the current workflow with you, agree on the rules, and decide what should happen when something does not fit the normal path.

  1. Map the routine

    Identify who does each step, what information they need, and where errors occur.

  2. Agree on rules

    Define triggers, approvals, notifications, and the cases that need a person to decide.

  3. Build and check

    Test the normal path and common exceptions with realistic examples before launch.

  4. Hand over clearly

    Document how it works and show your team where to review activity or resolve issues.
